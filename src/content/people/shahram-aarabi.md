Dr. Aarabi is a vascular surgeon interested in the care of underserved populations as well
as novel therapeutics for use in resource-limited settings. His research focuses on limiting
ischemic damage in traumatic limb injuries and on automated ultrasound monitoring of
critically ill patients.

He is Associate Professor of Surgery in the UCSF East Bay Surgery Program, and at Alameda
Health System's Highland Hospital he serves as Chief of Vascular Surgery and Assistant
Trauma Director. He is board certified by the American Board of Surgery in general surgery,
surgical critical care, and vascular surgery.
